Questions

Which banks does it work with?

It does not rely on per-bank templates. It looks for the pattern every statement shares: a date, a description and one or more amount columns. That covers most statements from major banks and card providers. The preview is free, so you can see whether yours converts cleanly before paying.

Does it work with scanned or photographed statements?

No. The PDF needs real text in it, which is what you get when you download a statement from online banking. A scan is a picture of text, and reading that needs OCR, which this tool does not do.

What do I get when I pay?

An unlock key. Enter it once and this browser converts unlimited statements in full. Keep the key: you can enter it again on another device or after clearing your browser data.

What if it gets my statement wrong?

Try the free preview first: the first rows show exactly how your statement will convert. If you paid and it does not work for your bank, ask for a refund.

Should I double-check the output?

Yes. Compare the last balance and the row count with your statement before relying on the file for tax or accounting. Layouts vary, and a tool that reads documents automatically can misread an unusual one.
